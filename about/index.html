About

I'm Taylor Madrinich, a Twin Cities guitarist with nearly 20 years of playing for other people's songs — live, in the studio, and as the fill-in when a band's guitarist can't make it. I hold a B.A. in Music Business from North Central University.

My approach is simple: show up ready and serve the song. I learn the set before the first rehearsal, run a rig that works every night, and play the parts that make the artist sound better.
